# ANDOR ANALOG — walnut finish

_30 October 2026. Visual only: no DSP, parameter, preset, state or behaviour change._

ANDOR Analog is the walnut sibling of ANDOR OSP's teak:

- walnut cheeks;
- the family's warm ivory ceramic;
- aged copper in place of OSP's coral;
- charcoal and graphite in the dark wells.

OSP keeps its own finish exactly. This is a product theme, not a global change.

## How it is built (opt-in, in the shared code)

- `osp::plugin::design::Materials` (andor-ui `Design.h`) holds:
  - the accent family;
  - the ceramic of the cards and panels;
  - the look-and-feel accent and layer A's colour and identity;
  - `graphiteOnWell`;
  - an optional skin pack whose pieces replace the shared ones by name.

  OSP's values are the defaults (`Materials::osp()`).
- The tokens drawing code already uses read the current materials (`colour::accent`,
  `colour::cardTop`, `palette::accent`, `palette::layer (0)`, `colour::identity (0)`). No
  drawing code knows which product it is drawing. They are constant-initialised references
  into one materials object, so OSP draws with exactly its old values.
- `skin::Pieces` consults the materials' pack first. `find`, `draw`, `composed` and
  `decode` answer from it for the names it has; each pack keeps its own cache.
- Two opt-in finishes for the dark wells:
  - knobs: the slider property `graphite` (charcoal cap, muted bronze rim, cream pointer,
    dark track);
  - `SegmentedControl` / `ValueSelector` `setOnDark`: the chosen key is the accent's dark
    tone with an accent keyline.
- `ProductInfo::materials` (andor-shell): ANALOG sets it. `ShellProcessor` applies it when
  constructed, before any window.
- **Scope:** a product's materials apply to its whole binary. Every instance of ANALOG is
  ANALOG; OSP and ANALOG are separate binaries, each with its own copy. No instance changes
  another's look (tested: two windows render pixel-identically).
- **Tests:** `design::ScopedMaterials` gives a scope its own materials. The visual regression
  renders a product's parity states with OSP's materials, so it still proves the shell draws
  OSP's interface pixel for pixel; the product's finish appears in the shipped render.

## Resources

`product/Resources/walnut-skin.zip` (about 340 kB), embedded by `juce_add_binary_data` as
`AnalogMaterialsData`. It is generated by `tools/make_walnut_skin.py` from OSP's
`ceramic-skin.zip`:

- **`housing`:** OSP's rendered housing, with its teak cheeks replaced by procedural
  American walnut:
  - seeded, so the file is the same every run;
  - continuous vertical grain with varied ring widths, broad figure, pores and fibre;
  - a rounded-post form lit from the front, with a satin sheen on the inner edge;
  - the render's own shading at the ends and in the recess.

  The ceramic, silhouette and geometry are untouched.
- **`badge-A`:** layer A's badge recoloured from coral to aged copper (`#c86a3c`), with the
  render's light and shadow kept.

No photograph or third-party texture is used. Everything new is computed by the script; the
rest is the family's own rendered assets. The handoff's concept images are not in the
repository.

## Deviations from the approved concept (deliberate)

| Difference | Why |
|---|---|
| Cheeks are about 22 px wide, not about 40 px with a dark recess beyond the instrument | The spec keeps the frame silhouette and the outer content rectangle. Wider cheeks would change the housing geometry shared with OSP. Owner decision. |
| No studio backdrop around the instrument | The plugin window is the instrument; the concept is a product photograph. |
| The workbench keeps its Quick / Detailed layout (no live waveform beside the editor, no wave arrows, no pair row in Quick) | Behaviour is the source's (the approved Oscillator Workbench). The concept is visual guidance only. |
| Macros keep their identity arcs (MORPH rust …); the concept shows copper and a simplified macro row | Macro colours carry meaning (modulation, LEDs). The six macros and their names stay. |
| ON is an LED key, not a copper checkbox | Same control, same behaviour; it already reads on dark. Restyling would be a new control. |
| The ceramic is the family's bone ivory, not slightly greyer | Family DNA; the spec's target `#E7DECF` equals OSP's housing face `#E5DED0` within a few levels. |

## Owner decisions (small)

1. Widen the cheeks (a housing geometry change that OSP would not share)?
2. Copper value arcs on the macros instead of their identity colours?
3. A slightly cooler, greyer ceramic for ANALOG only (`cardTop`/`panelTop` in its materials)?

## Proof

- **OSP:** full suite, including design capture and the visual references; the walnut
  tokens never reach it.
- **ANALOG:**
  - `[materials]`: the tokens are copper; the pack replaces only `housing` and `badge-A`;
    OSP's materials come back exactly within a scope and are not overridden by a new
    instance; two windows render the same; the session holds none of it;
  - the visual regression's parity against OSP;
  - every other product, DSP and legacy test.
- **Screenshots:** `andor_analog_tests "[screenshots]"` with `ANALOG_SCREENSHOT_DIR`. They
  are checked in Xvfb on Linux, not in a DAW or on macOS/Windows displays.
- **No code changed in** `dsp/`, `AnalogParameters`, the presets, the migration, MIDI, or
  the plugin IDs. `product/golden/parameter-schema.txt` is unchanged.
